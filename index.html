QuickQR Privacy Policy

Applies to the QuickQR browser extension (Chinese name: URL转二维码; formerly URL2QRCode) for Chrome, Microsoft Edge and Firefox. Last updated: October 7, 2026.

Summary

QuickQR does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. QR codes are created entirely on your computer.

What the extension accesses

  • Current tab address and title (activeTab permission). When you click the QuickQR icon, the extension reads the address and title of the current tab to show them and turn the address into a QR code. This happens only after you click, stays inside your browser, and is not saved.
  • Text you type (storage permission). Text you enter in the Text tab is saved as a draft in your browser's local extension storage, together with which tab you used last. It stays on your device until you click Clear or remove the extension. It is never sent anywhere.

Network requests

QuickQR makes no network requests. It does not use analytics, ads, tracking or any online QR code service. The Alipay and WeChat payment codes shown in the support panel are images bundled inside the extension.

The only exception is the Buy Me a Coffee button: if you click it, your browser opens buymeacoffee.com/harriswxy in a new tab. That website has its own privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

QuickQR 隐私政策

适用于 Chrome、Microsoft Edge 和 Firefox 上的 QuickQR 浏览器插件（中文名：URL转二维码；原名 URL2QRCode）。最后更新：2026 年 10 月 7 日。

概要

QuickQR 不收集任何个人数据，不把数据存到任何服务器，也不出售或分享任何数据。二维码完全在你的电脑上生成。

插件会读取什么

  • 当前标签页的网址和标题（activeTab 权限）。你点击 QuickQR 图标后，插件读取当前标签页的网址和标题，用于显示并生成二维码。只在你点击后读取，只在浏览器内使用，不会保存。
  • 你输入的文字（storage 权限）。在“文本”标签里输入的文字，会作为草稿保存在浏览器本地的插件存储中，同时保存你上次使用的标签。这些数据只留在你的设备上，点击“清空”或卸载插件即删除，不会发送到任何地方。

网络请求

QuickQR 不发起任何网络请求，不使用统计、广告、追踪或任何在线二维码服务。支持面板里的支付宝和微信收款码是插件自带的图片。

唯一的例外是 Buy Me a Coffee 按钮：如果你点击它，浏览器会在新标签页打开 buymeacoffee.com/harriswxy，该网站适用其自身的隐私政策。

政策变更

如本政策有变更，新版本会发布在本页面，并更新“最后更新”日期。
